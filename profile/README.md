# Povar

AI partner for SMEs in Portugal. We find where AI saves time and money,
build the solution and train the team to use it.

## How we work

1. **Listen**: a 30-minute conversation about how the business runs today.
2. **Measure**: a diagnosis of where time and money are being lost, with the client's own numbers.
3. **Solve**: training applied to real processes, and a pilot built around them.
4. **Support**: monthly monitoring, maintenance and improvement.

## Stack

Claude, n8n, Supabase, Next.js, Cloudflare.
